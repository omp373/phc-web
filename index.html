The Essence of Pula In Botswana, "Pula" is more than just our currency or the word for rain. It is a blessing. It is a greeting. It is the lifeblood of our land. It represents prosperity, unity, and the enduring strength of the Tswana people.

The PHC Vision The Pula Heritage Collection (PHC) bridges the gap between ancestral pride and modern luxury. Based in Francistown, we reject "fast fashion" in favor of permanence.

Our use of premium embroidery is a conscious choice. By weaving our emblem into the fabric, we mirror the commitment to our heritage: deep, lasting, and unmistakable.

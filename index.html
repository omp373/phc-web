The Meaning of Pula In Botswana, "Pula" is more than just our currency or a word for rain. It is a blessing. It is a greeting. It is the very lifeblood of our land. It represents prosperity, unity, and the enduring strength of the Tswana people.

The PHC Mission The Pula Heritage Collection (PHC) was born from a desire to bridge the gap between ancestral pride and modern luxury. We don't believe in fast fashion; we believe in permanence.

By utilizing premium embroidery, we ensure that the PHC emblem isn't just printed on fabric—it is woven into it. This mirrors our commitment to our heritage: deep, lasting, and unmistakable.
